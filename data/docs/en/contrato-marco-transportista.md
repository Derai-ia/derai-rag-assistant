# Transport framework agreement
Nordia Logística S.L. and Transportes Bruma Azul agree to transport palletized goods nationally. The standard delivery window is 24 to 48 hours from collection, except on public holidays or in cases of force majeure. The supplier confirms each pickup in the TMS.
## Confidential commercial terms
The fictional negotiated rates are 84 euros per standard pallet on mainland routes and 119 euros on the urgent route. Annual review is limited to the agreed index, with 60 days' notice. The commercial schedule is confidential and authorized for management and operations only.
## Service level and penalties
A delay attributable to the carrier of more than 24 hours may result in a credit of 3% of the affected freight charge. Lost goods require notification and a claim file. Incidents must be reported within a maximum of five business days.
