# Information security policy
Use individual accounts with multifactor authentication and lock your session when leaving your desk. Do not share passwords or export customer data to personal devices.
## Incidents
Immediately report suspicious emails, lost devices, or unusual access to the security team. Preserve evidence and do not reply to the sender. The security team acknowledges receipt during business hours.
