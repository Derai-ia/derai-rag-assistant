# Supplier onboarding
Request the legal business name, fictional tax ID, bank certificate, current insurance, and operational contact. Procurement verifies the documents and compliance before creating the supplier in the system.
## Approval
Operations validates capacity and coverage. Management approves strategic suppliers. Onboarding is completed within three business days after the file is complete; Procurement communicates the assigned identifier.
