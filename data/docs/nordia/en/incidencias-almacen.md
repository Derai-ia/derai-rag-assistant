# Warehouse incident guide
For damaged goods, separate the merchandise and photograph each package with its label visible. Record the location, shift, and shipment code. Do not destroy packaging until Quality closes the case.
## Inventory discrepancies
Count the location twice with a second person. Temporarily block the location and record the proposed adjustment. The shift supervisor approves any correction greater than five units.
