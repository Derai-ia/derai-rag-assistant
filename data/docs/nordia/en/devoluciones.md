# Returns procedure
The customer must request a return within seven calendar days after delivery and include the shipment identifier and reason. Support records the request and provides a return label once Operations authorizes the pickup.
## Receipt and closure
The warehouse inspects the returned package and records its condition in the TMS within two business days. Operations confirms the replacement or return closure and notifies Support.
