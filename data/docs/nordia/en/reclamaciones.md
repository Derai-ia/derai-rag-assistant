# Claims procedure
## Opening a claim
Record the incident in the internal portal within five business days. Attach the shipment number, packaging photos, and a brief description. Support assigns an identifier and acknowledges receipt within one business day.
## Resolution
Operations requests evidence from the carrier and updates the case every two business days. The resolution target is ten business days. If there is no response, escalate to the transport manager.
